GachiWork

An anonymous community for migrant workers in Korea.
AI-powered peer support, grounded in Korean labor law.

Group 18 · KLES Capstone · Interactive Prototype
Section 01

Onboarding

A purple-to-blue branded landing, a simple sign-in, and a 5-step sign-up that verifies the E-9 visa, captures a nickname, sets a password, and records nationality and preferred language.

Landing01
Hero with palette-driven gradient, feature preview cards, and dual CTAs for new and returning users.
Sign In02
Nickname or email + password, with password toggle and quick path to sign-up.
Sign Up · Step 103
E-9 visa verification — upload ARC photo, or enter registration number manually.
Sign Up · Step 204
Nickname + optional email, with a live avatar preview of how the user appears to others.
Sign Up · Step 305
Password with confirmation and a four-bar strength indicator.
Sign Up · Step 406
Nationality picker grouped into Southeast Asia and Central Asia & Other — the full E-9 sending-country list.
Sign Up · Step 507
Preferred language dropdown with privacy assurances and terms acknowledgement.

Default landing after auth — feed, AI teaser, FAB composer, and bottom tab bar with unread-count badge.
Section 02

Ask & Answer

From the home feed, a worker writes a post, gets an instant AI answer grounded in labor law + community data, and connects with peers who've had similar experiences.

Composer09
Post composer with topic chips, image attachments, and an anonymous-by-default toggle.
Post Detail + AI10
RAG-grounded AI answer, source citations, peer replies, and an "people with similar experience" cross-connect.
Category Board11
Industry-scoped board with member count, today's activity, and top-rated posts.
Explore12
Search with industry / topic / region filters, and quick-access boards by industry.
Section 03

Safety Net

When the AI detects a high-risk situation — unpaid wages, workplace injury, passport confiscation — it privately alerts the worker and offers a free, anonymous 1:1 consultation with verified experts.

Inbox13
Notifications and direct messages — risk alerts, AI updates, peer replies, and attorney conversations.
Risk Signal14
Private risk notice — only the user sees it. Explains the situation and offers clear next actions.
Expert Connection15
Free anonymous consultation with labor attorneys, lawyers, and Migrant Worker Support Center counselors in the user's language.
Profile16
Anonymous profile with activity history, trust temperature, and privacy settings.
